About Maya

Art that matters
to you

Maya is a Syrian-born artist living in Stockholm, with a Master's degree in Architecture and a lifelong passion for painting. Her architectural eye gives her work an innate sense of composition and intention.

She works across watercolour, ink, and acrylic — each piece painted entirely by hand. Her greatest joy is creating art that holds outsized meaning for the person who owns it: a beloved pet, a bouquet from a special day, a memory worth keeping.

Every commission begins with a conversation. Maya wants to understand what matters to you before she ever picks up a brush.

Currently shipping within Sweden only.

Maya doesn't sign her name — just a circle. Simple, unmistakable, hers.
